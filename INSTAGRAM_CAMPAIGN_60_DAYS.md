# Hopiin — 60-Day Instagram Pre-Launch Campaign

> **Goal:** Build hype, educate, and grow a waitlist in Ahmedabad (then expand) before the app goes live.  
> **Brand:** Hopiin · *Find people. Make plans. Hop in.*  
> **Colors:** `#020617` (bg) · `#8CFF4F` (primary) · `#4DB5FF` (secondary)  
> **Link in bio:** Landing site → `#waitlist` + `#circle`

---

## How to use this doc

Each day includes:
- **Format** — Reel / Carousel / Static / Story-only
- **Hook** — First line (stop the scroll)
- **Visual** — What to design or screen-record
- **Caption prompt** — What to write (adapt to your voice)
- **Story** — 2–3 story slides to post same day
- **CTA** — What you want people to do

**Weekly rhythm (default):**
| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Problem / Relatable | Feature / Product | Reel / Trend | UI / BTS | Community | Lifestyle / Ahmedabad | Recap / Poll |

**Posting time (Ahmedabad):** 7–9 PM weekdays · 11 AM or 7 PM weekends.

---

## Campaign arc (60 days)

| Phase | Days | Focus |
|-------|------|--------|
| **1 — The Problem** | 1–12 | Relatable pain. No product yet. |
| **2 — The Tease** | 13–20 | Something is coming. Mystery. |
| **3 — The Reveal** | 21–28 | Hopiin name, mission, landing site |
| **4 — Feature Drops** | 29–44 | One superpower per 1–2 days |
| **5 — The Experience** | 45–52 | UI walkthrough, screens, Hopiin Circle |
| **6 — Social Proof** | 53–56 | Future Users, numbers, testimonials |
| **7 — Countdown** | 57–60 | Waitlist push, launch tease |

---

## Brand voice cheatsheet

- **Tone:** Confident, warm, slightly cheesy (like your notifications), never corporate.
- **Audience:** 18–28 in Ahmedabad who want spontaneous plans, not another dead group chat.
- **Do:** Short sentences. Emojis sparingly (🔥 ✦ 🌙). Local references (CG Road, riverfront, chai).
- **Don't:** Say "revolutionary" or "disrupting." Don't over-promise dates.

**Recurring CTAs:**
- `Link in bio → join the waitlist`
- `Link in bio → join the Hopiin Circle`
- `Comment your city if you want Hopiin next`
- `Save this for when plans actually happen`
